About Sohaib Hussein Ali Al-Bayati

Sohaib is a passionate and driven engineering student, set to join the prestigious MIT Class of 2029. With a deep love for problem-solving and innovation, he has consistently demonstrated excellence in mathematics and science. His exceptional analytical skills earned him 1st Place in the American Mathematics Competitions (AMC), highlighting his ability to tackle complex mathematical challenges with precision and creativity.

Beyond academics, Sohaib is a dedicated learner who constantly seeks to push boundaries, whether through research, engineering projects, or competitive problem-solving. He is committed to making a meaningful impact in the world through technology and scientific discovery. With an insatiable curiosity and a relentless work ethic, Sohaib is poised to become a leader in the field of engineering, shaping the future through innovation and ingenuity.
