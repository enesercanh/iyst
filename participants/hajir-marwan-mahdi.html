Hajir Marwan Mahdi Al Azzawi

Hajir Marwan Mahdi Al Azzawi

Hajir Marwan is a dedicated high school student, born in 2009, currently studying at Secondary Palestine High School in Baghdad. Passionate about artificial intelligence, computer science, and general scientific knowledge, she actively seeks out new opportunities to expand her skills and expertise.

Hajir has participated in several prestigious programs, earning multiple certifications. She received a certificate from The New York Academy of Sciences, where she collaborated with diverse individuals to identify and propose solutions for common issues in Iraq. Additionally, she was awarded a certificate from the Smithsonian Youth STEM Exchange Program, where she explored topics related to environmental justice. Furthermore, she earned recognition in CODEAVOUR 2022, the largest AI and coding competition organized by STEMpedia.

Hajir aspires to deepen her knowledge in AI and computer science, contribute to technological advancements, and leverage innovation to address real-world challenges. She is eager to continue learning, collaborating, and making a meaningful impact in the field of science and technology.
