Manar Shihab Ahmed Al-Fallooji

About Manar Shihab Ahmed Al-Fallooji

Manar Shihab Ahmed is a 19-year-old dental student at the American University In Baghdad. She possesses a profound interest in scientific topics and harbors a great passion for the field in general. Among the various scientific disciplines, her primary interests lie in medicine, physics, and space exploration.

Manar has actively participated in scientific research, co-authoring a paper on nuclear energy with a group of peers from the IYST team, focusing specifically on nuclear waste and its disposal methods. This collaborative effort reflects her commitment to addressing critical issues in the field. Additionally, she has contributed to medical articles that were published on the GNG website, earning her a certificate of recognition for her efforts.

Manar firmly believes that science is the key to understanding everything in life. She is committed to pursuing her studies and contributing to the scientific community, aspiring to make a meaningful impact through her work. Her dedication exemplifies the idea that knowledge not only enlightens but also empowers individuals to innovate and solve the challenges of tomorrow.
