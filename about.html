About Us

The Iraq Youth Scientist Team (IYST) is a dynamic and innovative organization dedicated to empowering the next generation of scientists, engineers, and innovators in Iraq. Our mission is to foster a culture of curiosity, creativity, and collaboration, enabling young minds to tackle the challenges of tomorrow and contribute to the advancement of science and technology in Iraq and beyond.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate, and support young scientists by providing them with the resources, mentorship, and opportunities they need to excel in their fields. We aim to create a vibrant community of thinkers and problem-solvers who are passionate about making a positive impact on society.

Our Vision

We envision a future where Iraqi youth are at the forefront of scientific and technological innovation, driving sustainable development and improving the quality of life for all. Through our programs and initiatives, we strive to build a brighter future for Iraq and the world.
