Enes

About Enes

Enes is an ambitious and driven individual with a passion for mathematics, research, and innovation. He is the founder of "We Change," a nonprofit organization that provides extracurricular activities for young people in Iraq. He has also participated in Model United Nations, earning honorable mentions, and was selected for the prestigious Yale Young Global Scholars program.

Enes is dedicated to problem-solving, having competed in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) and working toward a gold medal. He is also working on various research projects, including renewable energy solutions and improving public transportation in Baghdad. With his relentless drive and vision, he aims to create impactful changes in education, science, and technology.
